For help, bug reports, or feedback, email dosmallnw@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Do not send payment card details.

Pro purchase is missing

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account used for purchase. If products do not appear, check your connection and try again later. You can manage active subscriptions in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

About the flights

Your Airway turns focus time into progress on a virtual route. The displayed flight is not a live airline service and does not reserve a real seat.

Your data

Your focus and flight history is stored on this device. If you delete the app, this local history may be removed. See the Privacy Policy.
