Privacy Policy

Your Airway · Updated 19 October 2026

Your Airway does not require an account. Flight history, preferences, cabin choices, and any aircraft artwork you create are stored on your device. We do not operate an analytics or advertising service for this app.

Information you provide

The app stores your focus sessions, virtual flight progress, departure and seat choices, and settings locally so it can restore your journey. If you select a photo for an aircraft design, the app receives only the selected image through Apple's photo picker and saves a local copy for that design. You can remove the design in the app or delete the app to remove local data.

Maps and purchases

The app uses Apple's MapKit to display maps; Apple may process map requests under its own privacy policy. In-app purchases and subscriptions use Apple's StoreKit. Apple handles payment information and tells the app whether you have an active entitlement. We do not receive your card details.

Device features

If you choose to use app blocking features, the app requests Apple's Screen Time authorization. Your selection stays under Apple's Screen Time controls and is used locally to apply the limits you chose. The app does not ask for your live GPS location; the suggested departure airport is based on device region settings.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information or send your flight history or selected photos to our own servers. Information stored by the app remains until you remove it using app controls or delete the app. Apple may retain purchase records under its own terms.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes and will date revisions here. For privacy questions, email dosmallnw@gmail.com.
