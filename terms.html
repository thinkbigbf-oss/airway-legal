Your Airway is a focus tool with simulated flights. Routes may be based on historical connections; flight progress, cabins, and other passengers are simulations. The app does not provide live flight status, sell tickets, or arrange travel. Apple's Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement applies to your use of this app.

Subscriptions and lifetime purchase

Airway Pro may be purchased weekly, monthly, or yearly as an auto-renewing subscription, or through a one-time lifetime in-app purchase. The purchase screen shows the price in your local currency, the billing period, and any eligible trial before you confirm. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Apple charges your Apple Account. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.
